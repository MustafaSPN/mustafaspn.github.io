## Who I am

Astro Flow Jam is an iPhone and iPad puzzle game published on the Apple App Store by Mustafa Sepen, an individual developer based in Türkiye. In this policy, “I” means the developer.

For privacy questions or requests, email [mustafa@sepen.dev](mailto:mustafa@sepen.dev).

## Your game data

You do not need an account, sign-in, or registration to play. I do not operate my own servers.

Your saved progress, coins, boosters, lives, settings, and chosen avatar are stored only on your device. Separately, Google receives the gameplay events, advertising information, and crash reports described below. Keeping your saved game on your device does not mean that all information about your use of the game stays there.

## Advertising: Google AdMob

The game uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, to show interstitial ads between levels starting from level 20. You can also choose to watch a rewarded video for an extra life or to double a level reward.

AdMob may collect device identifiers, your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and information about ads you see or interact with. Google uses this information to serve and measure ads, personalize ads subject to your permissions and consent choices, and prevent fraud.

“Remove Ads” removes interstitial ads between levels. Optional rewarded ads remain available, and choosing to watch them still involves AdMob's data processing.

Read more in [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Tracking permission and ad choices

After you complete level 3, the app makes a one-time request through Apple's App Tracking Transparency system for permission to track. AdMob uses the IDFA, Apple's advertising identifier, only if you allow this permission. This permission supports advertising that may involve activity across other companies' apps and websites.

If you decline, ads can still appear, but AdMob does not use your IDFA to personalize them. Declining does not stop every form of data collection needed to serve or measure ads or prevent fraud. You can change the app's tracking permission in iOS Settings.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google's User Messaging Platform displays a consent form before ads are requested. You can manage your advertising consent choices through Google's consent controls in the app. These choices are separate from Apple's tracking permission.

## Gameplay analytics: Google Firebase Analytics

I use Firebase Analytics, provided by Google LLC, to understand gameplay and balance level difficulty. It collects:

- Gameplay events, including levels started, completed, or failed; level numbers; time spent; progress; boosters and continues used; and coins spent.
- Rewarded and interstitial ad views, ad revenue, and in-app purchase events.
- Device model, operating system version, app version, and approximate country derived from IP address.
- An app-instance identifier generated by Firebase.

I use this analytics information to improve the game, not for advertising. Firebase's advertising-related consent settings—ad storage, ad user data, and ad personalization—are set to denied.

The app does not collect your name or email through gameplay, and Firebase analytics is not linked to a name or email. However, installation identifiers can connect events from the same installation; this information should not be understood as necessarily anonymous. Firebase information is not used to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

## Crash reports: Google Firebase Crashlytics

I use Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google LLC, to find and fix bugs. It collects crash reports and diagnostic information, including stack traces, device state, and a crash installation identifier. This information is not linked to your name or email and is not used for advertising or tracking across other companies' apps and websites.

For more information about both Firebase services, see [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## Purchases: Apple

Apple processes in-app purchases through the App Store. I do not receive your payment card details. In-app purchase events are also recorded in Firebase Analytics as described above.

Apple's processing is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## If you contact support

If you email me, I receive your email address and message. I use them only to reply to your request. Your support email is separate from the identifiers used by Firebase; emailing me does not by itself identify your analytics records.

## Retention and deletion

Your saved game stays on your device until it is erased. In the game's Settings, **Delete Account** permanently erases all locally stored game data on that device. Despite its label, this button resets local data; the game has no online account. It does not delete information already sent to Google or Apple's purchase records.

Firebase Analytics is configured with a 14-month data-retention setting. This setting is not a promise that all Google data is deleted exactly 14 months after collection: Google explains that aggregated reports are outside this setting and that identifier retention can depend on the activity-reset setting. See [Google Analytics data retention](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/7667196).

Crashlytics and AdMob have separate retention practices; the Analytics setting does not control them. Their retention information is available in the Firebase and Google policies linked above. Apple manages its records under its own policies.

You can request deletion of analytics data by emailing [mustafa@sepen.dev](mailto:mustafa@sepen.dev). Because analytics records are not linked to your name or email, locating the relevant installation records may require additional information. Deleting local game data alone does not submit this request.

## Children

Astro Flow Jam is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children, is not in Apple's Kids category, and is not intended for children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game or support, please contact me so I can investigate and address the request.

## International users and privacy rights

Google and Apple provide their services internationally. Their policies explain their international processing practices. Using the game is not, by itself, consent to every international transfer or use of personal information.

If the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to your information, you may have rights to access it, correct it, request deletion, restrict or object to its use, and receive a portable copy where applicable. You can withdraw consent where processing relies on consent; withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. These rights have legal conditions and exceptions.

If the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, applies, you may have rights to know and access personal information, correct it, request deletion, opt out of its sale or sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising, and exercise your rights without unlawful discrimination. Rights concerning sensitive personal information apply where relevant. An authorized agent may make a request where the law allows.

Personalized advertising can involve “sharing” under California law even without a payment for the data. This policy does not claim that advertising data is never sold or shared as those terms are legally defined. Use the advertising choices described above and contact me about privacy requests. Apple's tracking permission and California sale-or-sharing rights are separate concepts.

Contact [mustafa@sepen.dev](mailto:mustafa@sepen.dev) to make a request. Whether particular information can be provided or deleted depends on the applicable law and whether the relevant records can be identified.
